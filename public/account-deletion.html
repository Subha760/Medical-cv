Delete a Medico referral profile

  1. Open Account and sign in with your verified email.
  2. Select “Delete Medico profile” and confirm.
  3. Your profile, credit ledger, unlocks, edit sessions and support tickets are removed. Local CV files and the shared ChoiceMatrix sign-in identity remain.

Request sign-in identity deletion

Email subhajitsatpathi6@gmail.com from the account email with the subject “Medico account deletion”. Specify whether you want the shared ChoiceMatrix sign-in identity deleted, too. The owner will verify the request without asking for your password, explain any impact on other ChoiceMatrix services and respond within 30 days.

Local drafts and retained records

Deleting an online profile does not remove local CVs, photos, signatures or workspace data. Remove them in Saved CVs and Settings, or clear the app/browser's site storage after exporting anything you need. A salted completion fingerprint remains to prevent repeat referral awards. Technical security logs and backups follow provider retention policies; legally required records may be retained.
