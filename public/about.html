Built around a healthcare working day

MedCV brings healthcare CVs and a nursing workspace together. Build a structured CV, review a PDF, plan a rota, track credential dates and organise study notes.

A useful free starting point

The app offers 64 free original templates in eight categories, guided CV questions with Mira, local PDF generation, cover letters, saved drafts and daily tools. Thirty-two original premium designs are available through verified referral credits.

Clear boundaries

Mira and the 15 local assistants use rules and writing templates. They are not trained language models, and they cannot verify credentials, diagnose conditions or make treatment decisions. Proofread every output and check an employer's requested CV format.

Who operates the service

ChoiceMatrix operates MedCV (Medico). The support and privacy contact is help@choicematrix.in. See our career guides for practical ways to use the app.
