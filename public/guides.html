Practical healthcare career guides

Original guides for nurses and nursing students using MedCV. These are career and organisation tips, not clinical treatment guidance.

  • Build a clear nursing CV — Start with the role you are applying for, then choose relevant evidence.
  • A nursing student’s first CV — Show learning and readiness without overstating independence.
  • Keep a rota that works for you — A rota is useful when its codes and dates are unambiguous.
  • Track professional credentials — A simple expiry list helps you prepare early.
  • Choose a professional CV layout — Select structure for the application, not just a colour.
  • Edit an existing PDF or Word CV — Treat an imported document as a reference and review the result carefully.
