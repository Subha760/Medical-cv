Document import limitations

Supported inputs are PDF and DOCX, up to 8 MB, and at most 20 PDF pages. PDF edits add or cover positioned text while preserving reference pages; covered text can remain underneath in the file, so this is not secure redaction. Do not use white covers to remove confidential information. Scanned PDFs require manual text positioning. DOCX editing extracts text and rebuilds a clean Word layout; complex formatting, tables and images may not be preserved. Password-protected and legacy DOC files are unsupported.
