Contact & support

For app problems, privacy requests, accessibility feedback and template questions, email help@choicematrix.in. Include your app version, device type and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, authenticator secrets or patient information.

Verified account holders can also send a support ticket from the app's Account page and read the response there. Requests are handled by the owner through Pulse.

For a reserved edit that failed before finalization, include its session ID so the owner can review a one-time unused-edit refund. Completed exports cannot be refunded through the unused reservation tool.
