Privacy & storage policy

Effective 9 October 2026. MedCV (Medico) is operated by ChoiceMatrix. Contact help@choicematrix.in for privacy questions.

Local drafts and offline cache

CV drafts, photographs, signatures, saved CVs, shift plans, learning cards, task lists and preferences are stored on your device using browser storage. App files and public template previews are cached for offline access. Clearing site storage or uninstalling the app can remove this data. Export backups before clearing it. On a shared device, sign out and clear local drafts when finished.

Optional verified accounts

When you choose to sign in, Supabase processes your email, password authentication, session and security information. Passwords are managed by the authentication provider and are not visible in Pulse. Our private database stores an account ID, referral code and attribution, credit ledger, completion hashes, premium-template unlocks, edit reservation metadata and support requests. The administrator can view account and entitlement metadata, pause referral accounts and answer tickets. No CV library is available to the administrator.

Explicit document processing

Ordinary CV editing and PDF exports run locally. If you select “Verify” for referral completion, or enable the optional automatic first-CV verification setting in Account, your name, professional title, email and education or employment institution are sent to the service to validate completeness. Automatic verification happens when you download a qualifying CV while signed in; it is off until you consent and you can turn it off anytime. Photos, signatures, address, birth date and the rest of your CV are excluded; only a completion hash and receipt are retained. Account credit balances refresh from the server while the page is visible and when you return online. Paid PDF/Word import exports send the selected reference document and edit instructions to the service. These are processed in memory and not intentionally retained as files or database content. The service stores input and final-version hashes and entitlement records. Infrastructure providers may retain technical request logs, such as timestamps, IP addresses and error events. Do not include patient-identifiable information in documents or support messages.

Referral fairness

A verified new account completing its first CV can earn its referrer one credit. Repeated completions do not earn additional rewards. A salted email-derived completion fingerprint is retained after profile deletion to prevent repeat referral abuse. One credit buys one permanent template entitlement or one 24-hour edit session producing one final version. This is an entitlement rule, not a guarantee against copying an exported document.

Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled because publisher IDs and approval have not been configured. If Google AdSense is activated on eligible web pages, Google may process cookies and device/network information under its advertising policy. A configured Google-certified consent management platform must provide the required choices before eligible ad requests. Referral, editing, clinical workspace and Pulse pages do not carry ads. Android does not load web AdSense; native advertising requires a separate AdMob and consent setup. Referral rewards are never tied to ad views or clicks.

Your choices and deletion

You can use free features without an account, deny optional advertising, delete local CVs in the app, clear site data, sign out, export a backup and delete your Medico referral profile from Account. This removes its credits, unlocks, edit metadata and support tickets, but leaves device drafts and the shared ChoiceMatrix sign-in identity. To request deletion of that identity or other retained records, follow the account deletion instructions. We respond within 30 days, subject to security and legally required retention.

Other information

The local assistants use fixed rules and templates, not hosted generative language models. They do not send prompts to an AI provider. We do not sell CV data or use it to target advertising. The service is intended for adults pursuing healthcare careers. Its career and study tools are not diagnostic or prescribing systems. Providers include GitHub Pages hosting, Cloudflare HTTPS/domain protection and owner email verification, and Supabase infrastructure, with the database in India. Pulse email verification uses Cloudflare Access, which processes the owner email and login/security information. It does not grant access to the owner’s Gmail inbox. Owner email sessions last at most 30 minutes and use separate tab session storage. Provider security logs and backup retention follow their platform policies.
