Choose a professional CV layout

Select structure for the application, not just a colour.

Start with the essentials

For systems that parse CV text, start with a simple single-column design, standard headings and ordinary fonts. No template can guarantee compatibility with every tracking system.

Make the structure clear

Academic and detailed clinical applications may need more space for research, training and presentations. Use deliberate section order and a readable page break.

Check the details

Photos and personal demographic fields are optional and may be inappropriate for some employers or jurisdictions. Read the application instructions before including them.

Review before sharing

Premium layouts offer visual variety, but the evidence in your CV matters most. Preview the completed document on a phone and print-sized view.
