Build a clear nursing CV

Start with the role you are applying for, then choose relevant evidence.

Start with the essentials

Use a short professional summary that names your registration, current experience and the setting you want to work in. Avoid calling yourself an expert without evidence.

Make the structure clear

List clinical placements and jobs separately when that helps a reviewer understand what was supervised training and what was employment. Include dates, organisation, ward or specialty, and responsibilities.

Check the details

Describe achievements you can substantiate, such as a completed quality improvement project or teaching contribution. Never include patient names, case identifiers or confidential hospital metrics.

Review before sharing

Proofread phone and email details, verify registration dates, and use the employer’s requested file type. Preview every page before sending.
