A nursing student’s first CV

Show learning and readiness without overstating independence.

Start with the essentials

Include the qualification you are pursuing, institution and expected completion date. State registration status accurately rather than implying you are already registered.

Make the structure clear

Clinical placements can demonstrate exposure to specialties and teamwork. Explain tasks in the context of supervision and your scope of practice.

Check the details

Relevant volunteering, leadership, presentations and skills training can show motivation. Do not invent practice hours or certifications to fill space.

Review before sharing

A concise CV is usually easier to review than one filled with generic claims. Tailor each application and ask an educator or mentor to check it.
