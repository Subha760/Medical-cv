Keep a rota that works for you

A rota is useful when its codes and dates are unambiguous.

Start with the essentials

Import one reviewed row at a time, check the month and compare every shift against the official schedule. Split shifts should appear as separate time intervals.

Make the structure clear

Mark off days and leave clearly. Check overnight shifts on the following date and review short gaps or overlaps before exporting a calendar.

Check the details

Estimated hours are not an approved payroll calculation. Breaks, overtime rules, allowances and local law can change what is payable.

Review before sharing

Keep the official employer schedule as the authority. Export a backup before replacing a month, and keep shared calendars free of patient information.
