Track professional credentials

A simple expiry list helps you prepare early.

Start with the essentials

Record the credential name, issuing body, expiry and renewal lead time. Check the issuing body’s official requirements, including continuing development evidence.

Make the structure clear

Use reminders to organise action rather than assuming the app renews anything automatically. An expiry entry does not establish current legal registration.

Check the details

Store sensitive identity documents in a secure location. A CV generally does not need a full document scan or unnecessary personal identifiers.

Review before sharing

Review your list regularly and update the CV only after you have verified a renewal or new award.
