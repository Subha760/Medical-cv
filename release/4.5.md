# Medico and Pulse 4.5

Separate sign-in, account creation, email verification, password recovery and password reset pages replace the combined account form. Existing accounts can recover their password instead of repeatedly signing up. Login accepts existing shorter passwords; new passwords require ten characters and matching confirmation.

Medico persists its authenticated session on this device until logout, expiry or revocation. Logout uses local scope and retains CVs, cover letters and nursing workspace data. Account switching archives and restores each account's local drafts. Referral credits, unlocked templates and support records remain server-backed. Local drafts are not a cloud backup: export a backup in Settings before clearing browser/app storage or changing devices.

Pulse retains its owner session across tabs and restarts within the existing thirty-minute server-authorized window. Expiry and owner-only authorization remain enforced. Only harmless dashboard view preferences persist; reports are fetched after authorization. The Android Pulse app keeps the exact approved owner verification host inside its WebView and restricts native exports to trusted app pages.

A production signup probe was accepted with email verification required, then the temporary account was removed. This checks backend acceptance, not delivery or opening of a real inbox message. Production verification and recovery emails depend on Supabase's mail configuration. No owner credentials, passwords, signing keys or service-role keys ship in the app or source package.

The template limit stays at ten free templates. Premium templates and Mira continue to require verified server unlocks. Support: help@choicematrix.in. Ads remain disabled until valid configuration and approvals are supplied. Store approval and publication are not implied by these signed packages.
